Other languages

The caption you already wrote, carried into Spanish, French, Kreyòl or Portuguese — with the hashtag, the @ handle, the number and the brand name held exactly where you put them, and a check on everything that comes back.

This page does not translate. A laptop with no server has no dictionary, no slang authority and no idea whether a sentence sounds like somebody from Miami wrote it, so instead of printing a guess in a confident font it does the two jobs it can honestly do: it works out what a version may not touch, and it checks what a model hands back against your line. With no model connected it writes nothing in another language and tells you so — then gives you the sheet a person who speaks the language would need.

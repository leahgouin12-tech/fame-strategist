    What still needs the laptop

    • a new edit, a rendered preview, a clip cut — that is computed here, not stored, so the stored version is the one you last looked at;
    • fresh numbers, trends, and anything the agent has to think about;
    • the very first time you open a page after installing — pack once and it travels.

    The copy sits on your phone, behind your own screen lock, until you erase it here — changing the PIN or locking every phone does not reach into it, because by then the phone is not talking to the laptop. iOS also throws it out if the app goes about a week unopened.

    If the phone reaches nothing

    Windows blocks incoming connections by default, and a phone hotspot counts as a public network. Allow this port once, in a PowerShell that says Run as administrator:

    netsh advfirewall firewall add rule name="Fame Strategist" dir=in action=allow protocol=TCP localport={{ port }} profile=any

    The laptop has to stay awake and on the same network as the phone. Let it sleep and the icon opens a page that cannot load — that is the trade for the files never leaving this machine.

    What this is not. Packed, it is your whole plan on a plane — every page and picture the app can show, read on the phone with no network. It is not a laptop in your pocket: a new edit, a clip cut, a fresh number or an agent answer is computed here, so those wait until the phone can reach this machine. The PIN is a door on your own network, not a login system — five wrong guesses bring {{ cooldown }} minutes of waiting, and only you can change the PIN, from this page, on this laptop.

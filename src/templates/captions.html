Caption tones

The same shot, six temperatures. Type what is in the frame and each tone writes three lines out of your words — cunty, lit, funny, niche, aesthetic, soft. Below them, the tags this app can account for, each one labelled with what put it there.

This page was rebuilt once because the lines were unusable. Three rules came out of that, and they hold now: a sentence says the tank, not the black ribbed tank — nobody recites the colour, the fabric and the item in one breath unless they are reading a listing back; every line is contracted, because it is and it’s are not the same person typing; and the shapes that have circulated as templates for a decade — me: I’ll do it once, also me:, does X or does everything, obsessed, that’s the point — are dropped and counted, not printed with an apology beside them.

No model call: the lines are built on this machine out of the sentence you typed. Nothing here is filtered — an adult line is written when you ask for one, and the app names the word a caption filter is likely to look at instead of refusing to write it.

There is no trending-tag feed here because there is nothing to read. No platform publishes a hashtag list this app can query, and a scraped one would be somebody else’s account doing the ranking. A tag with no evidence behind it is not shown at all — the four sources that can put one on this page are labelled in the middle column. Banned-by-experience tags (#fyp, #viral, #foryou) are dropped even when they are already in your own captions.

What this page will not do

It will not soften what you asked for. The lines are written as spoken — the app has {{ n_watched }} words in its own watch list, and it says which one landed in which caption instead of quietly removing it or refusing to write the post.

That list is ours, not a published one: no platform prints the words its caption filter looks at, so the honest version is a note about reach, not a claim about breaking a rule. What this page cannot see at all is the crop, the sticker over the caption and the audio — those are yours to check before it posts.

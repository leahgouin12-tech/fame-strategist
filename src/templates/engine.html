What a key changes, and what it does not. With one, the coach can answer the question you actually asked rather than the nearest question in its rules, the niche brief reads your answers instead of matching them, and the face page describes the crop it was shown. Without one, all of those pages still work: they fall back to the researched rules and label the result as such. A key never unlocks an action this app would otherwise refuse — it does not post, follow, message or scrape anything on your behalf, and no model gets to decide that for you either.

To get a key: open the Google AI Studio page in your browser, sign in, and use its Get API key control. The free tier is enough for this app; nothing here is set up to spend money.

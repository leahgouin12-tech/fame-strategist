Followers

Both platforms will hand you your own follower and following lists — the official download-your-data export, no password to this app, no login, nothing scraped. Upload that file twice, weeks apart, and the difference is real churn: who left, between which dates, and what your own ledger says you were posting in that window.

Where the file comes from

  • Instagram Settings and privacy › Account ownership and control › Download your information › select Followers and Following › request a download. You get JSON files like followers_1.json.
  • TikTok Settings › Account › Download your data. You get following.json and a followers file; the following list also carries the date you followed each account.
  • Any platform If you would rather not wait for the file, paste the names — one per line — from the list on your screen.
The file is read on this machine and only the handles go into this app’s own database. Uploading the same list twice is not a bug: the second one is what makes a diff.

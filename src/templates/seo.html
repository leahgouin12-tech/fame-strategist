Being found

Two questions, answered from files already on this machine: which words a search could land on for you, and whether the post in front of you actually says them — and in the caption or only in a tag. Every phrase on the page comes with the thing that put it there.

This is not a keyword tool and cannot be one. No platform tells an app with no server and no login what a phrase is searched for or where her post sits for it, so nothing here shows a volume figure, a rank or a competitor’s text. A number of that kind would be an estimate of queries nobody ran; the list at the bottom of this page names exactly what is absent instead of leaving it implied.

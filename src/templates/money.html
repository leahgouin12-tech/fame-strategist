Money

A brand asks what you want. This page answers in the only three numbers you actually have: the median views of the posts you imported, a published cost per thousand that names where it came from, and what the post costs you in your own hours. Then it reads the caption for the word that legally has to be in it.

Nothing here speaks to a brand, finds a deal, signs anything or converts a currency. The bands are published ranges that disagree with each other, so they stay separate rows instead of being blended into one friendly figure, and a number whose source has gone stale or left the library drops off the page rather than being shown with a dead citation.

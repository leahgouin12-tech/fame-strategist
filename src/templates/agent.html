What this agent will never claim

It cannot post, schedule, follow, like, comment, message or read your account, and it will not tell you it did. There is no login in this build, no browser driving your sessions, and no platform API wired to a $0 local app. The strongest thing it does is stage a folder per day so posting is a drag instead of six copies and four guesses — and say so in the file it writes.

It also will not pretend to know what your face or body does to reach. The judge measures light, sharpness, placement and steadiness, and stops there.

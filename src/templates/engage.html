What this page will not do

It will not reply for you. No auto-reply, no scheduled comment, no DM to a stranger, no batch action across accounts — those are the parts of “grow my account” that get accounts restricted and readers distrustful, and a reply you did not write stops being a reason anyone follows.

It will not invent the middle of a sentence. Where the answer needs a price, a size, a stock status or something you saw on another person’s post, the line keeps a ____ and says what belongs in it.

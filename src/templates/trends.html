How the next run happens

This app has no browser, no login and no scraper, so it cannot see what is rising. The agent does. Paste the brief below into a Qoder conversation and it will open sources, reject the ones it cannot stand behind, and write what survives into data/playbook as dated entries this page reads back.

What this page will not do

It will not hand you a hashtag list — a trend that is one to three days old is already spent, and chasing it by tag is the part of “growth” that turns a post into a copy. It will not tell you a posting time; that is measured from your own ledger, not from a chart. It will not invent a finding to fill this screen: an empty page with a reason is worth more than a confident sentence with no address behind it. And nothing on it touches an account — every link opens in your browser, from your machine, by your click.

Clips

Drop the video files you are deciding on into {{ folder }}, or pick one below. Each one is opened with the ffmpeg that came with this app — five frames pulled out and measured by the same judge that scores your photos, plus four things only moving pictures can show: whether anything happens in the first two seconds, whether the clip moves at all, whether the frame drifts while it is held, and whether there is anything to hear. Then it is scored out of 10 and opened in the editor.

mp4, mov, m4v, mkv, webm, avi — decoded here, never uploaded anywhere.

It judges the clip, not you — and not your face. Nothing here measures a person. The editor cuts, straightens, reframes, brightens and fades what the camera already recorded. It will not slim a face, reshape a body, smooth skin, or generate a frame that was never shot — ffmpeg has filters that do exactly that, and this app will not call them.

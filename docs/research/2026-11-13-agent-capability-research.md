# Capability research — growth mechanics, appearance judging, official APIs
Online research 2026-11-13. Tiers: **[measured]** peer-reviewed · **[official]** platform's own words · **[industry]** named-data vendor · **[folk]** no citable source.
Tested locally: system + `.venv` = **Python 3.15.2 win_amd64**.

## 1. Growth mechanics

- **[measured]** Signal order **follows > likes > view-rate**, sock-puppet audit of TikTok. Boeker & Urman, WWW 2022 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12271 (Jan 2022). Only published ordering — and it is about feed personalization, not video distribution.
- **[official]** TikTok: follower count is *not* a factor ("Number of followers … are not used as factors"). Inputs = watcher history (watch-through, likes, comments, shares, saves, "Not Interested"), video info, device/account settings. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you (18 Jun 2020) · https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content
- **[official-adjacent]** Instagram's three named signals: **watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach** — Mosseri, repeatedly, incl. 23 Oct 2026. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DFFyRp-pINJ/ (Jan 2025) · https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/ (Nov 2026). **Contested: no published weights.**
- **[folk]** "Test batch / 200–500 view pool" — no platform source describes a view quota. Nearest official mechanism: Instagram **Trial Reels**, reels "shown to non-followers first," reporting views/likes/comments/shares · https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/ (10 Dec 2024). Third parties report a ~1,000-follower floor that would lock out a 0–1k account (https://metricool.com/instagram-trial-reels/) — **contested**, Meta states none.
- **[official]** Account trust exists only as *negative* gating: "ineligible for the For You feed" + appeal · https://support.tiktok.com/en/safety-hc/account-and-user-safety/content-violations-and-bans; under-16 and suspected paid-political ineligible · https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity. No documented positive warm-up score — **[folk]**.
- **[official]** Originality is a reach penalty: "reducing the reach of unoriginal content" · https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/ (13 Mar 2026). TikTok flags third-party watermarks [industry] · https://influenceflow.io/resources/why-is-my-tiktok-unoriginal-content-flag-appearing-and-how-to-fix-it/ (2 Oct 2026).
- **[industry]** Buffer, **11.4M posts / 150,000+ accounts**: 2–5 posts/wk → up to **17% more views**, 11+/wk → up to **34%**, but **median views stay flat** — lift is in the tail, more tickets not better tickets · https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/ (8 Oct 2025). "3–5×/day" is **[folk]**.
- **[measured]** 400 labelled TikTok videos, logistic regression **AUC 0.93**: "the number of followers is the most powerful predictor," then close-up/medium **shot scale** (framing, not facial geometry). Ling, Blackburn, De Cristofaro & Stringhini · https://arxiv.org/html/2111.02452v1 (3 Nov 2021).
- **[folk→industry]** "71%/87% decide in 3 seconds" has no traceable primary source · https://dynamoi.com/learn/tiktok-music-promotion/what-is-the-3-second-rule-on-tiktok (26 Apr 2026). What holds: completion/average watch time is a named input [official] — treat "hook" as a proxy for watch-through. Retention-by-length benchmarks are **[industry]**, mutually inconsistent, **contested** (https://www.go-viral.app/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026/, 16 Feb 2026 vs https://retensis.com/blog/category/guide, 26 Apr 2026).
- **[folk]** Niche consistency "3.2× faster growth" traces only to a blog citing Socialinsider, no primary report · https://www.go-viral.app/blog/how-to-find-your-tiktok-niche/ (15 Mar 2026). **Do not repeat as fact.**
- **[industry]** "Algorithms prioritize engagement over audience size"; TikTok engagement 3.70–3.73%, +49% YoY · https://sproutsocial.com/insights/organic-reach/ (16 Oct 2026).

## 2. Judging appearance vs viral

PyPI queried 13 Nov 2026, on 3.15.2 win_amd64:

| pip | ver | Python | Windows wheel | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mediapipe` | 1.2.3 (11 Nov 2026) | 3.9–**3.14** | `py3-none-win_amd64` | **works today**, CPU/XNNPACK |
| `opencv-python` | 5.0.0.93 | 3.6–3.14 | `cp37-abi3-win_amd64` | fine (abi3 covers 3.15) |

**Verified here:** `pip install mediapipe` succeeded on 3.15.2; `import mediapipe` + Tasks Vision worked; `face_landmarker.task` = **3,758,596 B (3.76 MB float16)**; `detect()` ran on CPU ("Created TensorFlow Lite XNNPACK delegate for CPU") in **0.30 s**; 478 landmarks + 52 blendshapes; correctly found no face in a blank frame. First `create_from_options` **30.7 s** cold — warm at startup. Footprint **364 MB** (matplotlib, `opencv-contrib-python`, `sounddevice` dragged in) — real on 3.66 GB RAM.
**Version trap:** MediaPipe officially stops at 3.14, onnxruntime at cp314. MediaPipe installs on 3.15 only via a `py3-none` wheel — luck, not support. **Pin the venv to 3.12/3.13.** On 3.15 every ONNX route (InsightFace, tiny FaceDet ONNX) is dead without a compiler.

**Appearance-vs-engagement research does not exist.** No peer-reviewed or platform-published work ties measured facial symmetry, facial thirds, canthal tilt or jawline definition to TikTok/Reels engagement or follower growth. Nearest real papers, and why each fails to support the feature:
- **[measured]** Face *presence*, not shape: +**38%** likes, +**32%** comments · https://news.gatech.edu/news/2014-03-20/face-it-instagram-pictures-faces-are-more-popular (20 Mar 2014).
- **[measured]** Image **symmetry + contrast** raise liking; N=361 and **610 Instagram posts** · https://www.emerald.com/ejm/article/55/6/1747/99600/ (4 Feb 2021). Compositional, not facial.
- **[measured]** Loucas et al., *ASJ Open Forum*: proportions of 100 top beauty influencers vs golden ratios — "ideal Instagram face is symmetrical, matching the golden ratios" · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39524229/ (10 Oct 2024). **Descriptive; never regressed on followers or engagement.**
- **[measured]** Liu et al. 2023: canthal tilt / uniform facial thirds separate "aesthetic" faces **as rated by people**, not platform metrics · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10335162/.
- **[measured]** Baylor 2018: high selfie *proportion* lowered perceived likability · https://news.web.baylor.edu/news/story/2018/followers-likes-attractiveness-increase-social-media-likability-selfies-not-so-much.

A landmark model can *measure* symmetry honestly; nothing licenses *predicting* reach from it.

**ToS.** TikTok US ToS (**15 Jul 2026**) bars bots/scraping/data extraction without written approval · https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/terms-of-service/en. Meta ToU bars "automated means" collection, cross-referenced to **Automated Data Collection Terms**, which state acceptance "alone does not constitute the required written permission" · https://help.instagram.com/581066165581870/ · https://www.facebook.com/legal/automated_data_collection_terms. TikTok **Research API** closed to her: eligible entities are "academic institutions in the U.S., EEA, UK, Canada, or Switzerland" plus qualifying non-profits, non-commercial, no redistribution of user content · https://developers.tiktok.com/products/research-api/ · https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/terms-of-service-research-api/en (eff. 25 Oct 2024). Counterweight: **Meta v. Bright Data** (N.D. Cal. 2024) held logged-*out* scraping of public pages did not breach ToS — scraper not then a "user" · https://digitalcommons.law.scu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=3834&context=historical. **Contested**, appealed, US-only, irrelevant to platform enforcement (they still ban you). Non-API automation bans are routine; prohibited is access "outside the sanctioned API surface" · https://openhosst.com/blog/tiktok-automation (12 Jul 2026). **Clearly fine:** her own content, her own analytics, her own manual screenshots for private analysis, TikTok **oEmbed** (`https://www.tiktok.com/oembed` — no token, no registration, no stated rate limit, embed HTML/metadata only) · https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/embed-videos/. **Not fine:** bulk-downloading other creators' videos/frames as a comparison corpus.

## 3. Official API reality

**TikTok Content Posting API** — `video.publish` scope. **Localhost OAuth explicitly allowed for desktop:** "Only `localhost` or loopback IP `127.0.0.1` are allowed host names in URI," max 10 · https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/login-kit-desktop/. **But** "All content posted by unaudited clients will be restricted to private viewing mode" and an unaudited client can "only post to a private account"; public direct post requires a TikTok **audit** · https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-get-started · https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post. Per-user token **6 req/min**; ~15/day (**contested** · https://postgate.studio/how-to/tiktok-api-rate-limit, 25 Aug 2026). App must be "Live in production," not Staging, for OAuth · https://stackoverflow.com/questions/73338099. **A $0 unreviewed app cannot public-post to TikTok.**
**TikTok Display / Video List** — `video.list` returns "a paginated list for the given user's public TikTok video posts"; 600 req/min per endpoint, sliding window · https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/tiktok-api-v2-video-list · https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/tiktok-api-v2-rate-limit (4 Aug 2026). **No creator analytics endpoint** — no watch time, retention, follows-per-view; counts only. **Her retention curve cannot be pulled programmatically.**
**Instagram Graph API publishing** — professional (Business/Creator) account required; Facebook-Login flavour needs a linked Page and cannot publish until that access completes; `/media` container → `/media_publish`, `REELS` type, **JPEG only image format**, **100 API posts/24h**, status via `fields=status_code` · https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing. Review required to post "on behalf of accounts you do not own"; for her own account a **Development-mode app with her as admin works without full review** · https://postproxy.dev/blog/post-to-instagram-via-api/ (28 Feb 2026) — **contested**; `http://localhost` redirects auto-allowed *only in Development mode*, rejected once Live · https://stackoverflow.com/questions/62422540.
**Instagram Insights** — impressions, reach, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, engagement; "User Metrics data is stored for up to 90 days" · https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/insights. Pollable with a long-lived token; **webhooks need a public HTTPS callback**, which a no-server laptop cannot serve.
**Instagram Messaging API** — professional account; reply within **24 h** of last message, or one **private reply within 7 days** to a commenter; `instagram_manage_messages` + review · https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/ (28 Sep 2026) · https://www.postmcpai.com/instagram-messaging-api (6 Sep 2026). Localhost DM automation is **out**.
**Instagram Basic Display API is dead** — unavailable since **4 Dec 2024** · https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024-09-04/update-on-instagram-basic-display-api/. **No official read path for a personal, non-professional Instagram account.**

## 4. What I would build

1. **Self-reported analytics ledger + her own correlation engine.** Best value, zero ToS risk, negligible RAM. She keys in TikTok Studio / IG Insights numbers; app stores locally, runs within-account regressions — hook style, length, shot scale, cadence → views, watch-through, saves, sends. Required because **TikTok exposes no analytics API**: self-report is the only lawful path to her retention curve. Anchors: Boeker [measured], Buffer tail-not-median [industry], Ling shot-scale [measured].
2. **Composition/export gate, no faces.** Extend `src/media/judge.py`: length-vs-cut-rhythm (frame diffs via `imageio-ffmpeg`), first-3-frames motion and luminance delta, edge simplicity, blown/crushed highlights, caption length, watermark presence. Pillow/numpy only, measurable, aimed at inputs both platforms name [official].
3. **Instagram Reels publisher via dev-mode Graph API.** Needs Business/Creator account, Page + app-admin consent, `http://localhost` callback while Development, 100 posts/24h, JPEG-only. $0, no review. **Never promise TikTok auto-publish** — unaudited is private-only; audit unrealistic for a personal app.
4. **TikTok "prepare and hand off."** Emit file + caption + hashtags + first comment, open the app for manual upload, nudge timer. Compliant; sidesteps the audit gate.
5. **Local reference set from her own posts + oEmbed metadata only.** oEmbed gives embed HTML, title, dimensions, author for links she supplies — context without storing anyone's frames. **No** scraped face/video corpus.
6. **Optional, feature-flagged: MediaPipe Face Landmarker on her own face only**, for framing/stability diagnostics. Symmetry/thirds/tilt computable (3.76 MB, CPU, 0.30 s) but must read **"descriptive, not predictive."** Gate behind venv pin to 3.12/3.13, the 364 MB install, the 30 s cold load.

**Must NOT be promised:** that any facial measurement predicts reach (no such research exists); that TikTok auto-publishes publicly from an unreviewed app; that watch time or retention can be fetched via API (it cannot); that follower-based "algorithm trust" can be gamed; or that a viral-reference library of other creators' faces/frames is legal here.

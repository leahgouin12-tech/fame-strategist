claim: Safety gate rule for X, allow scheduled original posts but refuse bulk or scripted follow and unfollow, duplicated or near-duplicated posts, follow churn, and any coordinated exchange of likes, replies, reposts or follows.
mechanism: X's authenticity policy names restricting reach as a penalty and defines it as excluding posts from search, trends and recommended notifications and removing them from For You and Following timelines, so reciprocal-engagement behaviour costs distribution before it costs the account.

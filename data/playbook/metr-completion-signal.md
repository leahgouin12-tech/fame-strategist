claim: Cut the video to the length you can actually get watched to the end, because finishing is the one watch behaviour both TikTok and Instagram name in their own explainers.
mechanism: TikTok calls whether a user finishes a longer video a strong indicator of interest, and Instagram lists how likely you are to watch a reel all the way through among the predictions its Reels ranker scores, so predicted completion is computed per video and a shorter clip that ends cleanly scores better than a longer one that trails off.

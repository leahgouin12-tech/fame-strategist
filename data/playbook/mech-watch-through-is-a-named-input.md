claim: TikTok and Instagram both publish watching a video to the end as a ranking input, so cutting for completion outperforms cutting for likes.
mechanism: TikTok's newsroom says factors are weighted by value to the user and that whether someone finishes a longer video is a strong signal, above weak ones like shared country, while Instagram lists the predicted likelihood of watching a reel all the way through among the Reels actions it scores.

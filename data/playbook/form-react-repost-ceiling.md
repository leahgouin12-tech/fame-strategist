claim: Do not build the account on reacting to or reuploading other people's clips, because Instagram withholds recommendation from accounts that mostly repost without meaningful edits and TikTok pays only for content it classes as original.
mechanism: Both systems would rather surface the source than a copy, so a reupload competes against the original it borrowed from instead of against other newcomers of its own kind.

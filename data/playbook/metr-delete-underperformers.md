claim: Leave a flopped post up and never re-upload the identical file, because Instagram's head says removing a post does not change the reach of anything else you post, while re-editing the same moment with a new opening is what can earn a fresh run.
mechanism: The rankers judge each video on its own predicted interest rather than on the health of the grid behind it, Mosseri stated on 30 October 2026 that taking a post off the grid only costs that post a little reach via lost profile visits and called repeated re-posting a bad growth hack, TikTok's explainer says already-seen and repeated content is not recommended so a duplicate file gains nothing, and Instagram publicly denies suppressing content as leverage, which leaves the delete-to-recover ritual without any platform basis.

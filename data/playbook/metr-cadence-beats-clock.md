claim: Lock in a weekly posting volume you can sustain before you optimise the clock, because in Buffer's 11.4 million-post TikTok study views per post rose up to 17 percent at 2 to 5 posts a week and up to 34 percent at 11 or more, while no study publishes an effect size for posting hour.
mechanism: TikTok states that neither follower count nor a history of high-performing videos is a direct factor in the recommendation system, so with no built-in audience every upload is a separate draw and more draws lift the ceiling without lifting the floor, median views stayed flat at 459 to 506 across cadences while the 90th percentile jumped from 3,722 to 14,401 views, and the only variance decomposition available attributes 0.012 of engagement-rate variance to hour of day against 0.662 to the creator themselves.

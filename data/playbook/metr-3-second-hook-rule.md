claim: Lead with the payoff or the tension instead of an intro, but write to the fastest honest opening you can manage rather than to a three-second quota, because the three-second rule is advice, not a measurement.
mechanism: The figure has no traceable origin, writers of tool documentation label it a rule of thumb with no study attached, agencies report finding no credible published benchmark for hook rate at all, and the nearest real numbers are paid-media artifacts, TikTok internal research saying 90 percent of ad recall impact lands inside six seconds and Google's 2015 analysis of thousands of TrueView ads across 16 countries, neither of which describes organic scrolling.

claim: Run list and countdown decks at 11 to 20 slides rather than 2 to 7, but expect only about five percentage points of extra breakout rate while you are under 10,000 followers, where Eden measured 18.0 percent for 2 to 4 slides against 23.5 percent for 11 to 20.
mechanism: Each extra slide is another swipe that Instagram counts as retention, and only about 17 percent of carousels are that long, so the deck competes in a less crowded slot of the same surface.

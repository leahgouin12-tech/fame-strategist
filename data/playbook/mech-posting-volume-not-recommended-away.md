claim: On TikTok, posting more videos does not change how each one is recommended, but the feed pulls recommendations generally from videos posted in the last 90 days, so recency matters more than volume.
mechanism: TikTok states outright that the amount of videos you post will not impact how your content is recommended in the For You feed, while also saying recommendations generally pull from videos posted within the last 90 days, which makes a long silence, not a busy day, the thing that costs reach.

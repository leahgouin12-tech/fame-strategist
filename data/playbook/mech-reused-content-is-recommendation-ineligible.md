claim: Material you did not create or add anything to is held out of the recommendation feeds on both platforms, so re-uploaded clips can publish and still reach almost nobody.
mechanism: TikTok makes content ineligible for the For You feed if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new and still lets the video be found in search and on the profile, and Instagram excludes reels that repost others' work or carry watermarks, so the post stays published but is left off the surfaces that reach strangers.

claim: Spend the limited video minutes on Reels and put the depth in a carousel - Buffer's 2026 analysis of roughly two million posts measured Reels at 1.36 times the reach of a carousel and 2.25 times a single image, while the 2026 benchmark sets put median engagement per reach at about twice the Reel figure for carousels, reported as 6.90 percent against 3.31.
mechanism: Reach and engagement are bought in different markets. Reach is graded against strangers, which is the only market an account with no followers is trading in, and motion is what the distribution surface pushes to them. Engagement is graded among whoever already stopped, and a swipeable set gives a person who arrived several reasons to save and comment instead of one. So the Reel is the top of a funnel whose saves happen two taps later, not a rival format competing for the same slot.

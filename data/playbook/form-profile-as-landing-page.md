claim: Fix the profile before the first upload: pin up to three videos on TikTok that show the range and the thesis of the account, keep the pitch short enough to read at a glance, and expect no clickable TikTok link until 1,000 followers or a registered business account while Instagram gives everyone up to five.
mechanism: A viewer who taps through from a single video decides in seconds whether the account is worth a follow, and the pinned slots plus the link field are the only surfaces where the answer is written once instead of per post.

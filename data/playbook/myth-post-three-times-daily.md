claim: Treat any per-day posting quota as an experiment budget, not a rule; the only platform-published number is TikTok's 2021 ads guidance of 1-4 posts a day, and it was framed as a testing recipe rather than a ranking threshold.
mechanism: Blogger chains took a 2021 TikTok for Business creative-guide line written for advertisers with budgets, hardened it into growth advice, and inflated it to 3-5 a day as each post restated the last without checking the source.

claim: Safety gate rule, refuse any clip this account did not film or produce, because TikTok makes content ineligible for the For You feed when it carries unoriginal or reused material without anything new and can restrict an account from appearing in top search results.
mechanism: TikTok's distribution engine is the For You feed, so it filters reused footage out of recommendation and reserves account-level restrictions on posting, search placement and FYF eligibility for accounts that try to game the system.

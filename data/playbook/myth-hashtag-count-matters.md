claim: Use two to four descriptive hashtags and no more, treating them as labelling rather than distribution, because Instagram's own help page promises only placement on a tag page while the largest recent dataset found a negative association with views.
mechanism: A hashtag only routes a post to a tag page and gives the classifier a weak topical hint, so its measured effect depends on whether anyone still browses that tag and on whether heavy hashtag use selects for the low-reach accounts that lean on it.

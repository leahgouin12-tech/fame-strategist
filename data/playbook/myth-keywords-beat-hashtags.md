claim: Write the literal phrase a viewer would type into the first line of the caption and into the bio, since both platforms document caption and profile text as discovery inputs and neither makes that promise about hashtags.
mechanism: Instagram's reach guidance tells creators to put relevant keywords in content, caption, bio and hashtags, and TikTok lists captions, sounds and hashtags as the video information its recommender reads, so plain text is the one matching surface a new account fully controls.

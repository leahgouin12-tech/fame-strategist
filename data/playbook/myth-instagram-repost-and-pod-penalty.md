claim: Safety gate rule, block aggregator-style posts and any arranged comment or like swap, because Instagram gives extra reach only to accounts that had a role in producing the content and pauses recommendation for every post from an account that repeatedly breaches its guidelines.
mechanism: Recommendations put content in front of strangers, so Instagram holds recommended material to a higher standard than permitted material and names unoriginal repurposing with only minor edits plus coordinated comment networks as disqualifying patterns.

claim: A post is advertising the moment anything of value changed hands, so the disclosure has to use an ordinary word like advertisement, ad or sponsored, sit where it is hard to miss rather than behind a MORE fold, and for video appear in the video itself, not only in the caption.
mechanism: FTC staff guidance treats compensation as financial, employment, personal or family relationship with the brand, which pulls gifted product and long term deals into the same duty as cash. The guidance names advertisement, ad and sponsored as the terms that carry, requires the disclosure to be hard to miss and not buried, repeats it for every live stream rather than once at the top, and states that a platform's own paid partnership tool is not assumed sufficient: the person posting carries the visibility duty. The Endorsement Guides FAQ adds that the brand and its intermediaries share the liability for what the creator says and how clearly it is labelled, which is why a missing label is a contract problem and not only a style one.

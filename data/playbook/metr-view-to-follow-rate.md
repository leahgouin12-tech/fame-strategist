claim: Measure follows per thousand views on your own last thirty posts and use that as the only benchmark, because no platform publishes a view-to-follow rate and the percentages floating around are vendor guesses.
mechanism: The metric exists but the denominator does not, Instagram Reels insights report which Reels converted viewers into followers with no rate attached, the largest public TikTok corpus of 460,483 posts publishes no profile-visit or follow conversion figure at all, and the pages that do quote numbers label them as unverified working heuristics such as 0.15 percent of non-follower reach on Reels and 0.25 percent on TikTok, while Socialinsider's 67.5 million-post sample measures growth as a yearly rate by tier, showing accounts under 5K followers growing fastest at 22 percent.

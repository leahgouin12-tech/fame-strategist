claim: Choosing the narrower of two topics is a bet rather than a finding: the citable part is only that TikTok supply grew 72 percent in videos published while views fell 31 percent year on year, which is a saturation argument, not proof that niching wins.
mechanism: The folklore holds that a narrow topic is graded against fewer comparable posts and a more specific viewer set, which is plausible for a cold account and completely untested against the cost of running out of things to say.

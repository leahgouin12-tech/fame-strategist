claim: Build a recurring series only on top of a format that already holds retention, because every documented episodic win so far sits on brand production money or a pre-existing large audience.
mechanism: Recurring characters and open storylines turn one-off viewers into returners, and returning viewers only matter once the account has enough reach for the word returning to mean anything.

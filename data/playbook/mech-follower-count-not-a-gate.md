claim: Having no followers switches nothing off, because TikTok says follower count and past hits are not direct ranking factors, Instagram ranks reels on engagement rather than audience size, and X applies a new-author boost.
mechanism: All three score the individual post against predicted user actions instead of the size of its author's audience, and X's published pipeline lifts authors with few impressions toward a target position while decaying repeats from the same author.

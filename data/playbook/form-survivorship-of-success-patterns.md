claim: Before copying any pattern from a creator who made it, write down how many accounts used the same pattern and stalled, because posting more on TikTok lifted the 90th percentile of views from about 3,700 to 14,401 while the median stayed near 500 regardless.
mechanism: Only breakouts are visible enough to be studied, so their habits look like causes when the measured effect of the same behaviour is a change in the odds of getting lucky rather than in typical outcomes.

claim: Put the claim in the spoken first line instead of setting it up, and consider asking a question in the caption, since Instagram scores whether viewers pass the three-second mark and TikTok posts phrased as questions draw 26 percent more comments.
mechanism: A stated position gives a stranger one beat of argument worth staying for, and comments plus early retention are among the first signals a cold post is graded on before any audience exists to seed it.

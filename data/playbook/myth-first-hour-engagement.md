claim: Design the post so the reaction arrives fast, but do not chain yourself to the clock for sixty minutes, because early velocity is a real documented signal while the one-hour window is a blogger invention.
mechanism: Instagram ranks Feed and Explore partly on how quickly people like, comment, share and save, yet Metricool's 24 million post set puts most Instagram views in the first three days, so the reaction rate matters over a much longer runway than the folklore hour implies.

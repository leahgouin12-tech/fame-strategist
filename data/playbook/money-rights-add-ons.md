claim: Usage rights, whitelisting and exclusivity are priced on top of the base fee rather than inside it, and the published premiums run from a fifth of the base to a multiple of it, so a quote that stays silent about them is a discount she has already given.
mechanism: Impact.com's creator guide has most influencers add 20 to 50 percent of the base rate for usage rights and notes follower count and engagement move where in that range a quote lands. Socialync reports two published guides putting whitelisting at 20 to 30 percent of the base fee per month and a third at a flat $100 to $500. Lumanu's brand-facing guide describes the same permission priced differently, as a percentage of ad spend, offering 4 percent as an example, so a $50,000 media buy adds $2,000. Tokfluence sums the category at 15 to 300 percent of base for paid usage, whitelisting and exclusivity together and advises agreeing them before the base. Four sources, three pricing shapes: per month, per ad spend, per base.

claim: Cut the deep-dive where the curiosity turns instead of where the topic ends - the second named shift in TikTok's 2026 forecast is Curiosity Detours, audiences taking a thread several layers deep rather than going to one conclusion, which argues for a numbered series that follows the turn rather than a single compressed explainer that answers everything.
mechanism: An explainer that resolves in forty seconds leaves the viewer with nothing to return for, and an account with no followers cannot rely on the profile visit to convert one watch into a follow. A post that ends at the interesting complication hands the next view to the account, and the complication is findable in her own material as the moment she stopped summarising and started following the thread.

claim: Price a sponsored post as median views divided by a thousand times a cost per thousand views, because that is the only basis the published figures share, and the figures themselves span an order of magnitude.
mechanism: Kolect's 2026 benchmark puts the median at $15.46 per thousand first month views. Ramped's ledger of executed campaigns, recalculated on 30 September 2026, reports median CPMs of $4.46 in fintech over 11 campaigns, $5.24 in iGaming over 38, $6.69 in education over 3 and $25.71 in wellness over 3, and says outright that a vertical or country missing from the table means too few executed campaigns to publish a median. Influencer Advisory reads $40 to $53 per thousand views as the median YouTube sponsor CPM on channels above 50,000 subscribers from 552 creators, and OutlierKit ranges the same platform at $15 to $80 by niche. Four sources, four non overlapping ranges, all of them computed the same way.

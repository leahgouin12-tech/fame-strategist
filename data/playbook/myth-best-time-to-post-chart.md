claim: Pick one realistic window and hold it for three weeks of your own numbers rather than adopting a vendor chart, because the two biggest published charts name opposite days for the same platform.
mechanism: Buffer's 7.1 million TikTok posts make Sunday 9 a.m. the top slot and Saturday the best day, while Sprout's nearly 2 billion engagements call Sunday the weakest day and put TikTok peaks on Tuesday to Friday afternoons, and each only sees posts scheduled through its own tool.

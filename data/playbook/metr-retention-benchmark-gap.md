claim: Stop chasing a published retention target such as 65 percent at three seconds or 80 percent completion, because none is platform-published and the two measured corpora point opposite ways, so benchmark each new post against your own last twenty instead.
mechanism: The numbers circulating come from mismatched denominators, Wistia analysed over 13 million hosted videos and 79 million hours and reports shorter videos earn a higher share-watched engagement rate while cautioning a longer clip can still win total watch time, whereas a 460,483-post TikTok corpus finds engagement rate per view lowest under 15 seconds at 3.71 percent and highest over five minutes at 5.25 percent, so a single copied threshold is an artifact of whose dashboard it was read from.

claim: The other basis in use prices a post by follower tier rather than by views, and where the two bases disagree the view basis is the one her own numbers can actually evidence.
mechanism: Kolect's 2026 guide lists $250 for a nano account under 10,000 followers, $400 for micro between 10,000 and 100,000, $600 for mid tier from 100,000 to 500,000, $850 for macro from 500,000 to a million and $1,500 above that. Tokfluence publishes a TikTok tier card in the same shape, then notes that add ons move the price most, with paid usage rights, whitelisting and exclusivity adding 15 to 300 percent to the base fee and agreeing them before the base is agreed. A follower table is a guess about reach; the metrics she imports measure it, which is why this app quotes from the view basis and shows the tier basis only as the number a brand may open with.

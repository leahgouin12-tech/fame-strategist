claim: Leave the flop up and re-cut it into a materially different edit, or use trial reels to test first, because a straight re-upload lands in the duplicate bucket Instagram already suppresses.
mechanism: Instagram states it makes reels that have already been posted less visible and defines engaging content as work that has not been on Instagram before, so a re-upload inherits a penalty for the exact condition the creator was trying to escape.

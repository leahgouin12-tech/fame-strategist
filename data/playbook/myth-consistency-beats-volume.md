claim: Hold a volume you can sustain for six weeks and judge it by whether reach is still climbing, because the only firm platform statements cut against volume, not for it.
mechanism: Instagram says it tries not to show too many posts from one person in a row and that most people look at less than half of their Feed, Sprout's 3-billion-message set shows brand volume falling from 11 to 9.5 posts a day, and the vendor consistency numbers are correlations across accounts that already have audiences.
